# Google Business Profile — weekly posting kit

Log in at business.google.com. Each post takes about two minutes. Post on the
same weekday each week so it becomes routine, and always attach a photo or a
Short. Add `?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=post` to links so GA4 shows what the
posts bring in.

## One-time setup (do these first)

- **Service areas:** add Sterling, Ashburn, Leesburg, Gainesville, Burke, and Oakton to the existing list.
- **Services:** make sure Paint Protection Film, Ceramic Coating, and Window Tinting are listed with prices "from $1,800", "from $800", and "from $280". Remove Vinyl Wraps if it is listed.
- **Phone:** (703) 775-4383, matching the website.
- **Products tab:** add "Full Front PPF — $2,400", "Ceramic Coating — from $800", "Ceramic Window Tint — from $280", each linking to the matching service page.
- **Q&A tab:** ask and answer your own top five questions (copy from skylinecustomshop.com/faq) so customers see them before they ask.
- **Photos:** upload 10 job photos now, then 3–5 every month. Name files descriptively before uploading (e.g. `tesla-model-y-full-front-ppf-chantilly.jpg`).

## Reviews

- Text every finished customer within an hour of pickup: "Thanks for trusting us with the [car]! If you have 60 seconds, a Google review helps a small shop more than you'd think: [your review link]". Get the short link from the GBP dashboard under "Ask for reviews".
- Reply to every review within a day, including the name of the service ("Glad the full-front PPF on the Model Y turned out clean").
