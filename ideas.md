# Skyline Custom Shop — Design Brainstorm

## Response 1
<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Industrial Brutalism meets Automotive Precision

**Core Principles:**
- Raw, high-contrast visual language that communicates strength and durability
- Asymmetric layouts with hard edges and intentional tension
- Typography as a structural element — oversized, cropped, and commanding
- Every element earns its place; zero decorative filler

**Color Philosophy:**
- Deep matte black (#0A0A0A) as the dominant surface — the color of a freshly coated car
- Burnt orange (#E85D04) as the sole accent — heat, energy, precision
- Off-white (#F5F0E8) for text — warm, not sterile
- Emotional intent: power, craftsmanship, and uncompromising quality

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-bleed sections with hard diagonal cuts between them
- Text blocks anchored to left or right edges, never centered
- Services displayed as large horizontal "slabs" stacked vertically
- Pricing shown in a dense, editorial grid — like a spec sheet

**Signature Elements:**
- Diagonal slash dividers between sections (clip-path)
- Oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) as typographic anchors
- Thin horizontal rules used as structural separators

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Hover states reveal hidden information (price details, service descriptions)
- Scroll-triggered entrance animations: elements slide in from the left
- CTA buttons have a "fill sweep" hover effect left-to-right

**Animation:**
- Entrance: staggered fade-up on scroll for content blocks
- Hover: orange underline sweep on nav links
- Hero: slow Ken Burns zoom on background image

**Typography System:**
- Display: Bebas Neue — bold, condensed, industrial
- Body: DM Sans — clean, modern, readable
- Accent: DM Mono — for prices, specs, and data
</text>

## Selected Design: Response 1 — Industrial Brutalism meets Automotive Precision

This design philosophy was chosen because it most authentically reflects the nature of automotive protection work: precise, powerful, and uncompromising. The dark-on-orange palette directly aligns with Skyline's existing brand identity, and the industrial aesthetic communicates the craftsmanship and durability of the services offered. The asymmetric layout and editorial typography will make the site stand out sharply from competitors who all use generic, centered layouts.
